# Core — Images

Photos on spots: the upload endpoint with its checks and processing, the per-user storage quota as it is enforced on upload, signed image URLs, deleting images and their blobs, the upload rate limit and pause switch, and the gallery in the spot panel and the quota bar on the account page. The photos belong to [spots](./spots.md); the platform admin sets quota overrides, defaults and the uploads switch in [admin](./admin.md), and deleting a trip also removes its blobs ([trips](./trips.md)).

## Photos from the web and Wikimedia Commons

| ID | Rule | Status | Since | Changed by | Tests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C-IMG-13 | `POST /spots/:id/images/from-url {url}` (planner, board admin, owner; visitor 403, non-member 404) downloads an `http(s)` URL of at most 2000 characters on port 80 or 443 and stores it through the upload pipeline with the codes of `POST /spots/:id/images`. The API downloads with a TripBoard User-Agent, follows at most 3 redirects, gives up after 10 s (422 `fetch_failed`), stops after the caller's per-file limit (413 `{error: "too_large", maxFileBytes}`, C-IMG-21) and treats a non-2xx answer as 422 `fetch_failed`. It only connects to public addresses: every resolved and connected IP, also after redirects, must not be loopback, private, link-local, CGNAT, multicast, unspecified, IPv6 unique-local or an IPv4-mapped form of those, otherwise 400 `url_not_allowed`, and the connection uses the checked IP. Wikimedia links are rewritten or stored as Commons files first (C-IMG-22). It records `image.upload` with `metadata.source = "url"` in the same transaction. <!-- R-34-03, R-34-04, R-34-06, R-43-03, R-43-10, R-43-11 --> | active | 34 | 43 | T-34-01, T-34-02, T-34-03, T-43-06, T-43-08, T-43-09 |

## File size limit and Wikipedia links

| ID | Rule | Status | Since | Changed by | Tests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C-IMG-21 | Every image path caps a file at the user's effective `max_file_bytes`: the `users.max_file_bytes` override, else `app_settings.default_max_file_bytes` (10 MiB), both 1 MiB to 50 MiB (`CHECK`), exposed as `user_quota_usage.max_file_bytes`. `POST /spots/:id/images` reads it before the body and passes it as the multipart `fileSize` limit; a `Content-Length` above the limit + 2 MiB is 413 without reading the body, the route's own `bodyLimit` is 52 MiB while the global one stays 12 MiB, and `from-url` / `from-commons` stop their download at the same limit. Every such 413 is `{error: "too_large", maxFileBytes}` and stores nothing. <!-- R-43-01, R-43-02, R-43-03 --> | active | 43 | — | T-43-01, T-43-02, T-43-03, T-43-06 |
| C-IMG-22 | `from-url` turns Wikimedia links into what fits: an `upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/<project>/<h1>/<h2>/<name>` original (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, any case) or a thumb wider than 2048 px is fetched as its `2048px-` thumb, falling back to the original when that answers non-2xx, with the address checks of C-IMG-13 on every URL fetched and `source_url` = the URL the user gave; a `/wiki/File:` or `/wiki/Datei:` page on `commons.wikimedia.org`, `<lang>.wikipedia.org` or `<lang>.m.wikipedia.org` (or any `/wiki/…` page with `#/media/File:`) is stored like `from-commons` with license check, 409 `already_added`, credit and `metadata.source = "commons"`, and a file Commons does not know is 422 `fetch_failed`. <!-- R-43-10, R-43-11 --> | active | 43 | — | T-43-07, T-43-08, T-43-09 |
