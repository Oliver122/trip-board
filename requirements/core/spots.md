# Core — Spots

How a trip's spots are created, read, changed and deleted through the API and the spot panel, how addresses are geocoded, and how the board switches between spots. A spot is either a home spot or carries one or two [kinds](./kinds.md); stay periods and the primary home base are described in [home](./home.md). Spot photos are in [images](./images.md), searching, filtering and sorting the list in [list](./list.md), and how spots join plan entries in [planning](./planning.md). Roles and the guard come from [trips](./trips.md) and [members](./members.md).

## Places from Google Maps links

| ID | Rule | Status | Since | Changed by | Tests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C-SPOT-25 | `POST /trips/:id/places/from-link {url?, text?}` (planner, board admin, owner; visitor 403, non-member 404) takes the first `http(s)` URL of `url` or else `text` (each ≤ 2000 characters) and answers `{title, address, lat, lng, link, placed}`. Only Google Maps links are accepted (`maps.app.goo.gl`, `goo.gl/maps…`, `maps.google.<tld>`, `google.<tld>` and `www.google.<tld>` with a `/maps…` or `/search…` path); anything else is 400 `link_not_google_maps`, and more than 20 requests per user per minute are 429 `rate_limited` with `Retry-After`. A short link is followed with `HEAD` requests (at most 5 hops, 5 s in total, only Google hosts, `consent.google.com?continue=<url>` is replaced by its `continue` URL, no body read), and a bad hop, too many hops, a timeout or a non-2xx/3xx answer is 502 `link_resolve_failed`. With a name the point is matched through the geocode service (name plus address hint, then name; within 300 m of the link's coordinates, else the first hit inside the trip's box), otherwise the link's own coordinates with a reverse-geocoded address are used; with no point at all `placed` is `false` with `lat`/`lng` null, and a geocoder failure gives the link's coordinates and `address: null`. `title` is cut to 120 characters and `link` is the user's URL when ≤ 500 characters, else a `google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=…` URL. <!-- R-33-02, R-33-03, R-33-04 --> | active | 33 | — | T-33-02, T-33-03, T-33-04 |
| C-SPOT-26 | Pasting a Google Maps link on the board (Ctrl/Cmd+V while focus is not in an `input`, `textarea` or `contenteditable` and no dialog other than the spot panel is open; planner, board admin, owner only) shows the toast “Looking up place…” and opens a fresh add panel with title, address, link and the draft pin, and the map pans to the pin; other pasted text is ignored without a message. An open panel with unsaved changes first asks “Discard unsaved changes?”. A draft filled this way counts as a change, stays editable, and with `placed: false` leaves the pin unset and shows “Couldn't place this on the map. Click the map to set the pin.”. The texts and the errors `link_not_google_maps`, `link_resolve_failed` and `rate_limited` exist in `en` and `de`. <!-- R-33-05, R-33-07, R-33-11 --> | active | 33 | — | T-33-05, T-33-11 |
| C-SPOT-27 | The add panel (planner, board admin, owner) has a *Google Maps link* / *Google-Maps-Link* field with a *Fill in* / *Übernehmen* button above the address field; the button shows a spinner while the lookup runs, a typed title is kept while address, link and pin are filled, and failures appear under the field with their translated text. <!-- R-33-06 --> | active | 33 | — | T-33-06, T-33-10 |

## Known gaps

- C-SPOT-03: no DoD test covers the `plans` list in `GET /spots/:id` (R-10-08).
- C-SPOT-05: R-06-09 lists title, info and link limits only; the code also limits `address` to 300 characters (`packages/shared/src/spots.ts`), which no DoD test checks.
- C-SPOT-09: step 19 has no DoD test for the `spot.create` and `spot.delete` events (only T-19-02 for update); the rule is proven for update only.
